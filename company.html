Our Mission

OmniNStack builds enterprise AI platforms, not one-off AI consulting projects. A single shared AI infrastructure — orchestration, retrieval, and governance — powers a growing portfolio of enterprise AI products, starting with Convergo, our AI Customer Engagement Platform, and Contexa, our AI Trust & Safety Platform. Both run end-to-end on infrastructure our customers own, with absolute data sovereignty and zero vendor lock-in.

Government & Innovation Funding

Built to Qualify as a Technology Platform, Not a Services Firm

Canada's innovation programs draw a sharp line between AI platform companies and AI consulting services — we're positioned, and engineered, as the former.

IRAP (NRC Canada)

Canada's largest R&D funding program for technology companies. We're built and positioned as an AI platform company — not AI consulting — the distinction IRAP evaluates for eligibility. Engagement in progress.

Innovate BC

BC-based since incorporation, building enterprise AI infrastructure from Langley. Engaging with Innovate BC's ecosystem for grants, investor introductions, and commercialization support for BC technology companies.

SR&ED (CRA)

Our proprietary R&D — agent coordination, sovereign memory & retrieval, edge inference optimization — targets genuine technological uncertainty, not routine engineering. Full technical detail in our whitepaper. Documentation in progress.
