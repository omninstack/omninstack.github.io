Outward bridges the gap between everyday messaging apps and real-time local knowledge. Built directly into ubiquitous chat channels like WhatsApp, KakaoTalk, or Messenger, customers can interact with a virtual assistant without installing extra software.

Key Value Propositions

Outward runs on two purpose-built modules: Outward Studio for AI-driven content and copy, and Outward Chat for omnichannel messaging, in-chat commerce, and attribution.

📈 1. True Offline Attribution & POS Integration (Outward Chat)

Stop guessing your digital marketing ROI. Outward connects digital conversations to physical retail terminals.

  • Instant Coupon Redemption: Customers receive custom barcode/QR coupons directly in their messaging apps, which can be scanned and validated in real time at the counter or self-service kiosks (Clover, Toast, OKPOS, Force, etc.).
  • ROI Attribution: Automatically link physical in-store sales back to the specific digital message or campaign variant that drove the customer to the store.
  • Post-Purchase Automation: Automatically trigger follow-up surveys, digital receipts, or return-discount coupons via WhatsApp or KakaoTalk the second a transaction is registered at the POS.

🤖 2. AI-Powered Copywriting & Conversational RAG Search (Outward Studio)

Boost engagement and conversions using built-in LLM assistants — without the hallucination risk of an ungrounded chatbot.

  • Copywriting Copilot: Instantly generate multi-variant message copies, headlines, and call-to-actions (CTAs) optimized for different channels and brand voices.
  • Social Content Studio: Automatically generates and schedules multi-modal social posts (text, graphics, localized promotions) across major SNS channels, so businesses without a marketing team stay active on social media.
  • Directory & Catalog Recommendation Search: Allow customers to discover nearby physical stores or products via natural, conversational queries (e.g., "Find a Korean restaurant near me serving Jjajangmyeon"), powered by vector database searches (pgvector/Qdrant) and geographical coordinate filtering.
  • Database-Verified, Not Just Plausible: Vector search finds candidate stores/products; every candidate is re-checked against PostgreSQL for live price, stock, and sale status before it reaches the customer — the AI never states a price or availability it hasn't just confirmed.
  • Context-Aware Follow-Ups: "Is that still available?" correctly reuses the store/product already resolved in conversation. A new store, product, price limit, or category mentioned mid-conversation triggers a fresh lookup instead of a stale answer.
  • Alternative Suggestion Engine: If an exact item or location is unavailable, the AI automatically proposes the best nearby alternatives and asks clarifying questions to maintain engagement.

🔄 3. Omnichannel Delivery & Resilient Fallback Routing (Outward Chat)

Never lose a lead due to delivery issues. Outward Chat integrates directly into the communication apps customers already use — KakaoTalk, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, LINE, Viber, Zalo, WeChat, SMS, and RCS.

  • Automated Fallback: Configure rules like: "Attempt delivery via WhatsApp first; if the message is not delivered in 15 minutes, automatically send it via SMS."
  • Consent & Compliance Sync: Centralized, highly-available opt-out datastore (Redis) updates unsubscribe requests in real time across all channels, performing pre-flight consent checks to guarantee spam compliance.
  • Isolated Cloud Sandbox: Every channel integration and conversation runs inside a safe, isolated cloud sandbox, keeping customer data and channel credentials contained.

⚡ 4. Enterprise-Grade High Availability & Monitoring

Engineered from the ground up for high-availability enterprise environments. Our modern, cloud-native stack handles complex messaging workloads with low-latency stability.

  • Asynchronous Ingestion: Built using NestJS and distributed message queues (Kafka / RabbitMQ) to absorb sudden spikes in incoming webhook traffic without system slowdowns.
  • Resilient Job Processing: Async worker/callback delivery with retry and backoff for transient failures, plus stale/dead-job detection so no customer message silently disappears.
  • Real-Time System Alerts: Monitors system webhooks continuously; if an external provider callback times out, the system routes diagnostic telemetry and Telegram alerts to internal engineering admin tools.
  • Externalized Secrets & Config: Tokens, credentials, and environment configuration are never hardcoded — a standard security practice extended across every channel integration, including Kakao's callback secret-token verification.
