# Contexa: Autonomous AI Trust & Safety Platform

## The Challenge

Cyberbullying and online toxicity present unique operational challenges: they run 24/7 without geographic bounds, leverage anonymity for rapid spread, and inflict severe psychological harm on victims. Traditional moderation relies on slow, manual reviews or basic keyword filters that fail to catch nuanced, evolving harassment.

## The Solution: Contexa

Contexa delivers real-time, identity-aware content moderation and dynamic enforcement across modern platforms. Operating fully on-premise or air-gapped, Contexa monitors multi-modal communications to identify toxic patterns, collect court-ready digital evidence, and execute automated policy actions.

## Key Capabilities

* **24/7 Real-Time Behavioral Analysis:** Identifies toxic behavior, hate speech, and harassment across chat threads, forums, and image uploads before content goes viral.
* **Immutable Chain of Custody:** Automatically logs timestamped, tamper-proof evidence trails using identity-aware auditing for legal or administrative proceedings.
* **Policy-as-Code Enforcement:** Executes immediate technical remedies (such as shadow-banning, rate-limiting, or temporary account locks) using deterministic policy rules (OPA).
* **Absolute Privacy & Data Sovereignty:** Keeps sensitive user data, internal flag reports, and moderation history completely self-hosted, ensuring compliance with strict privacy regulations (GDPR, PIPEDA, HIPAA).
